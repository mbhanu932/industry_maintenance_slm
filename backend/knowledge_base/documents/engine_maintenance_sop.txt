INDUSTRIAL ENGINE MAINTENANCE SOP

1. GENERAL INVESTIGATION
- Always review predicted remaining useful life (RUL), health score, anomaly score, and risk level together.
- A high-risk classification should trigger engineering review.
- Do not diagnose a specific component failure unless supporting evidence is available.
- Compare current machine behavior with historical degradation trends whenever possible.

2. SENSOR INVESTIGATION
- Review the sensors contributing most strongly to abnormal behavior.
- Investigate sustained changes rather than isolated sensor fluctuations.
- Compare current sensor behavior with the machine's previous operating cycles.
- Anomaly scores should be interpreted together with RUL and health indicators.

3. MAINTENANCE DECISION
- If predicted RUL is decreasing significantly, prioritize engineering inspection.
- If health score is low, review recent telemetry and degradation trends.
- Maintenance actions should follow approved engineering procedures.
- The AI assistant provides investigation support and recommendations only.
- A qualified engineer must approve maintenance actions.

4. HIGH-RISK CONDITION
- High-risk assets should be prioritized for engineering review.
- Review recent telemetry behavior.
- Check for abnormal sensor patterns.
- Compare the current condition with historical degradation.
- Determine whether inspection or scheduled maintenance is appropriate.

5. SAFETY
- The AI system must not directly control or shut down industrial equipment.
- AI recommendations must be reviewed by qualified personnel.
- Do not perform maintenance solely on the basis of an AI-generated recommendation.
